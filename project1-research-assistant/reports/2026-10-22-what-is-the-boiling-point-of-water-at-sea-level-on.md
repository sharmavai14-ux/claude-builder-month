# Water's boiling point at sea level

## Short answer
Water boils at 212°F (100°C) at sea level, but this temperature decreases with increasing altitude due to lower atmospheric pressure.

## Details
- A liquid boils when its vapor pressure matches the air pressure around it.
- Water needs a higher temperature to boil below sea level and a lower one at high elevation.
- 100°C is the figure for normal air pressure at sea level. Weather that changes the air pressure can move it a little.
