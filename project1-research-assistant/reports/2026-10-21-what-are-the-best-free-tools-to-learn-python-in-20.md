# Best free tools to learn Python in 2026

## Short answer
The best free choice I could confirm is **Python for Everybody (PY4E)** by Dr. Charles Severance. It's free online, including as a 14-hour video on freeCodeCamp's YouTube channel. Other good free options are the official tutorials on Python.org, Codecademy's Python 3 course and Corey Schafer's YouTube videos. My search tool hit its limit early, so this list is short and comes mostly from pages written before 2026.

## Details
- **Python for Everybody (PY4E):** This is a beginner course with no prerequisites. It starts with installing Python, then covers variables, loops, functions, strings, files, lists and dictionaries. Later parts cover web data, databases and charts. Dr. Chuck's site says the course is on Coursera, edX and freeCodeCamp, and "free online for everyone."
- **Coursera is only partly free:** Each Python for Everybody course on Coursera says "Enroll for free." But the page for the full bundle of courses says you can't take it for free, and you have to pay for the certificate. If you can't pay, you can apply for financial aid. To avoid any cost, use the freeCodeCamp video or Dr. Chuck's own site.
- **Python.org:** A 2025 guide suggests the official Python website for its documentation and beginner pages. They come straight from the people who make Python.
- **Codecademy and YouTube:** The same 2025 guide lists Codecademy's Python 3 course, which has hands-on exercises, and Corey Schafer's YouTube videos, which go from beginner to advanced. I couldn't check whether Codecademy's free plan has changed since then.
- **A newer option:** "Python for Everyone" (technaom.github.io) is a free course with no sign-up. It has quizzes, projects and over 150 practice problems, and it teaches how to use AI coding helpers and spot their mistakes. It's small and lesser-known, so treat it as an extra, not your main course.
- **What I couldn't check:** I wanted to look at other well-known free options too, such as Harvard's CS50 Python course, freeCodeCamp's own Python certification and the free online book *Automate the Boring Stuff with Python*. My searches stopped working before I could, so I can't confirm their current status. Check their websites directly before you start.
